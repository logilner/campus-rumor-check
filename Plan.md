# Campus Rumour Checker
The end goal of this project is to close the gap between how fast rumors spread on campus (via texts, social media, and word of mouth) and how slowly offical confirmation or denial typically reaches students. By giving users a fast, sourced way to check a claim against verified outlets, the tool aims to reduce unnecessary panic and prevent the spread of false information. 

# Scenerio
This website can be used for students to verify something they read online or heard from a friend. Example: Student hears there is a fire in Abel Hall. They go onto the website, input "I heard there was a fire in Abel Hall", and the website outputs if this is factual or not, with sources from UNLPD, The Daily Nebraskan, etc. 

Additionally, this website can be used for another purpose. Let's say a reporter at The Daily Nebraskan has heard about something that just happened on campus and they want to see if another Lincoln news outlets has broke the story yet. They can use the website to see what other sources, if any, have reported on an incident yet.

# Ideal outcome
In the ideal outcome, a student hears a rumor, checks it on the site and, within seconds, gets a clear, honestly-labeled answer: confirmed by an official source, actively being reported on but not yet confirmed, or no matching information found at all. Each response has direct links to where that status came from. Over time, the site could also build a small archive of previously checked rumors, so recurring false claims (like repeated fake safety scares) get flagged instantly as having circulated and been debunked before. For student journalists, the same 
tool doubles as a quick way to see whether a story has already broken elsewhere before they invest time chasing it themselves.

# Resources
UNLPD Crime and Fire Log
https://scsapps.unl.edu/policereports/MainPage.aspx
    Official, Clery Act mandated log of crimes and fires reported to UNLPD. Best for safety-related rumors (fires, thefts, assaults). Note: entries are posted within 2 business days, so absence from the log doesn't necessarily mean a claim is false.

UNL Alert 
https://police.unl.edu/services/unl-alert/  
https://emergency.unl.edu/
  Official emergency notification system; fastest source for active/urgent safety incidents.

The Daily Nebraskan
https://www.dailynebraskan.com/news/
    Student newspaper; useful for campus news, events, and policy rumors not covered by police logs.

Nebraska Today
https://news.unl.edu/
    UNL's official university news source; useful for verifying official statements, announcements, or administrative decisions directly from the university.

Lincoln Police Department
https://www.lincoln.ne.gov/City/Departments/Police/Police-Activity
    For incidents occurring off-campus but still relevant to students living in Lincoln.
